# Design Decisions

## Domain Selection

The university club and event management domain was selected because it represents a real-world campus system involving students, clubs, events, and participation.

## Scope Decision

The ontology focuses only on club and event management. It does not include full university administration, academic systems, or payment processes.

## Membership Modeling

Membership is modeled as a concept because students are connected to clubs through membership. This allows future extensions such as roles, start dates, and membership status.

## Attendance Modeling

Attendance is included to represent student participation in events. It is important for answering competency questions about event participation.

## Future Improvements

Future versions may include:
- SHACL validation
- SPARQL queries
- Alignment with external ontologies
- Documentation using Widoco
